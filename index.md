## Understanding Your RO Membrane Lifespan
The RO membrane is the heart of your water purifier, responsible for removing up to 95% of dissolved solids and impurities. Understanding the typical RO membrane lifespan is crucial for planning maintenance and avoiding sudden breakdowns. In ideal conditions, a quality RO membrane can last between 2 to 3 years. However, in Jaipur's demanding water conditions, with high TDS and hardness, this lifespan can be significantly shorter. Factors that affect membrane longevity include the quality of your water, frequency of use, and how well you maintain the pre-filters. Regular servicing and timely filter changes are the best ways to protect your membrane and maximize its lifespan. When you notice a change in your water's taste, a drop in water pressure, or an increase in TDS levels, it may be time to check the membrane's health. Professional technicians can test the membrane's performance and recommend replacement if necessary. Investing in a high-quality membrane and professional installation ensures your system continues to deliver safe water for your family. The membrane works by allowing only water molecules to pass through while rejecting dissolved salts and impurities. Over time, these impurities accumulate on the membrane surface, causing scaling and reducing efficiency. This is why regular maintenance and timely replacement are essential for maintaining water quality and system performance.

## How to Clean RO Filter at Home
Regular cleaning of your RO system's filters is an important part of maintenance that you can do yourself, though it's important to know what you can and cannot clean. Learning [how to clean RO filter at home](https://www.rocareindia.com/ro-water-purifier-service-jaipur) can help you maintain your system between professional services. The pre-filters, which include sediment and carbon filters, can be cleaned to some extent. You can remove these filters and rinse them under running water to remove visible dirt and sediment. However, this is only a temporary fix, and they should be replaced regularly as recommended by the manufacturer. The storage tank can also be sanitized at home. You can drain the tank, prepare a mild bleach solution (one teaspoon of bleach in a gallon of water), and run it through the system. Let it sit for 15-20 minutes, then flush thoroughly with clean water. This process kills bacteria and removes biofilm. It's crucial to note that you should never attempt to clean the RO membrane yourself. The membrane is a delicate semi-permeable component that requires professional chemical cleaning or replacement. Attempting to clean it with chemicals or by scrubbing will damage it permanently. Always follow the manufacturer's guidelines and consult a professional for complex maintenance tasks. Regular home maintenance, combined with professional servicing, ensures your system operates efficiently. When cleaning your system, always ensure you use food-grade sanitizers and follow safety guidelines to avoid contaminating your water supply.
